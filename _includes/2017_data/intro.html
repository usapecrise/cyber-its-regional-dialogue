About the Dialogue

DATE
June 29–30, 2026
FORMAT
In-Person Dialogue
LOCATION
Bangkok, Thailand
Background

Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) offer significant benefits in safety, efficiency, and resiliency, but they also introduce increasingly complex cybersecurity risks that can disrupt transportation operations, impact trade, and erode trust in digital systems. As transportation sectors across APEC continue to undergo rapid digital transformation, governments and industry stakeholders must strengthen their ability to prepare for, prevent, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity incidents across aviation, maritime, rail, transit, and road sectors.

Purpose

Organized under the APEC Transportation Working Group (TPTWG), this regional dialogue will convene public and private sector stakeholders from across APEC economies to exchange experiences, share emerging practices, and explore practical approaches to strengthening cyber resilience across transportation systems. The event will help inform the development of a Transport Cyber Hygiene Toolkit and future regional efforts focused on transport cybersecurity preparedness and operational resilience.

Participants

The dialogue is intended for transportation officials, cybersecurity policymakers, technical experts, infrastructure stakeholders, and industry representatives involved in transportation security, digital infrastructure, and operational resilience across APEC economies.
